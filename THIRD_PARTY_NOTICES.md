# Third-Party Notices

## Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot Images

The tarot card faces are the 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith deck illustrated by
Pamela Colman Smith under the direction of Arthur Edward Waite.

The original deck is in the public domain. Display images are bundled locally
with the application under `assets/images/tarot/rws-1909/`, using optimized
copies derived from public-domain Rider-Waite-Smith card scans.

The previous runtime source was the Internet Sacred Text Archive's scan of
*The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1911):
https://www.sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/

The application does not modify the card artwork. Reversed cards are rendered
by rotating the corresponding upright card image at display time.
